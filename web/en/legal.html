Rules for playing with others

There are other real people in the game. So that everyone can enjoy it:

  • Treat others with respect: no insults, harassment, threats or hate messages.
  • No sexual or violent content, and no offensive player names.
  • Do not write personal data about yourself or others (phone numbers, addresses, social media, photos).
  • Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Do not spoil the game for others: no blocking streets with cars, no following someone who does not want you to, and no using programs to annoy people or to overload the server.
  • If you are under 14, play with the permission of your mother, father or legal guardian.

If someone breaks them, you can mute them (you stop seeing what they say) or report them from “Who is here”. When reported, they cannot speak for a while; and we can block access for anyone who keeps doing it.

How to tell us about something

If you see something illegal or against these rules, write to us at the address above saying what happened, where (the town, the street and the player name) and when. We will look at it as soon as possible and reply.

The townspeople in the game

What the townspeople in the game say is written by an artificial intelligence. They are characters, not real people: they can be wrong, and nothing they say is real information about the town or about anyone.
