Why 388 Pueblos exists

When a town empties, it isn’t only the people who leave. Its festivals go too, and its nicknames, its street games and its way of naming things. We build a living copy of each one, so it can be visited, played and lived in from anywhere in the world.

Discover

Churches, squares, chapels and streets rebuilt from public data and photography. The town’s memory, told by its own people.

Play

Quests, live festivals, challenges between neighbouring towns and a passport that gets stamped town by town.

Meet up

An open place for young people from here and for those who left: friends, cousins, grandchildren and curious people from anywhere.

Origins · Guareña, Badajoz

It all starts in a town in the Vegas Altas.

388 Pueblos is the idea of Ana Pérez Toledo, an entrepreneur from Guareña. Her generation spends hours in digital worlds that have nothing to do with where they come from. She asked herself why one of those worlds couldn’t be her own town.

That’s where the project comes from: to recreate, carefully and accurately, the reality and character of Extremadura’s towns in an open digital universe. So they are not forgotten, so they help us plan them better, and so anyone, wherever they live, can come in, play and stay.

“I don’t want my town to be just a memory. I want it to be a place you can come back to from anywhere.”

Ana Pérez Toledo · founder
The world · four layers

A real town, inside and out.

Each municipality is built in layers: data first, the game on top, people all around and, finally, decisions.

Digital twin

The town as it really is. Building volumes from the Catastro, LiDAR terrain from the IGN (Spain’s National Geographic Institute) and photogrammetry of façades, churches and corners. Measurable and easy to update.

Open and free

All 388 towns, open to everyone.

Free, always

Access to the 388 towns, and spending time with other people inside them, is and always will be completely free. So are many activities and games.

Today, everything’s free

And tomorrow, no walls

Right now, nothing costs money. Later on there may be some paid services or games inside a town, always alongside many free ones and without shutting anyone out. The project is funded by the town councils that choose to wake their town up.

      Questions

      What people ask us most.

      How much does it cost?

      For now, everything is free. Entering the 388 towns and spending time with other people will always stay free; in the future there may be some paid services or games inside a town, alongside many free ones.

      What does it take to wake my town up?

      Residents and a town council. Each town has its own queue: when it reaches 100 people signed up, we take that data to its town council so it can commission the work. Sign up by choosing your town, and tell your friends and family.

      Where can I talk to other players?

      On the 388 Pueblos Discord, with a channel for each district, the build log and a space to suggest ideas. You’ll find it in the “Community” section.

      Can I play now?

      Yes. We’re testing with a waiting list: sign up with your email and you get into the Guareña beta straight away.

      Can I edit my town?

      Yes. 388 Pueblos is open source: the code is licensed under MIT, and the towns and their content under CC BY-SA 4.0. In the repository we explain how to propose changes to your town. The goal is for you to be able to do it without knowing how to code, too.

      I’m from a town council. What do we do?

      Your town is already built automatically. Waking it up in detail (square, church, streets, quests and festivals) is done together with the town council: look at the options above or ask us for a presentation.

      What do you do with my data?

      We only store your email and, if you want, your alias and your town, to keep you posted about the project. We explain it in the privacy policy.
